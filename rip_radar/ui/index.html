Channels

Each store's alerts go to its own channel. Pokémon Center queue gets an @everyone the moment the queue is up, a live "up for X min" message, and how long it lasted. News articles go to the main channel, so store channels stay products-only. Topps calendar gets Topps release-calendar changes, reminders and a pinned list of every upcoming Topps drop; Topps products gets the per-format pings (Mega, Blaster…). Drawings & raffles gets every drawing, lottery and invite from all stores, and each one also posts in that store's channel. Drop calendar gets every announced date, a pinned 14-day schedule and an 8 AM rundown (it only posts if you set it). App status gets start-up checks, problems and update notices. Leave any other box empty and those alerts go to the main channel. Saved on this PC for good, including through updates.

What to track

This computer

Off: clicking X quits Rip Radar and alerts stop. On: X hides it to the tray by the clock and it keeps scanning (right-click the tray icon to quit).

Text messages (Twilio)

Optional. Urgent alerts also arrive by SMS. Needs a Twilio account and number (about $1–2 a month plus under 1¢ per text).

"Still running?" chat bot

Optional. Type "still running?" in Discord and the bot answers with status. Create a bot at discord.com/developers, turn on Message Content Intent, invite it to your server, then paste its token here.
